Vidhai AI — Account Deletion

This page explains how Vidhai AI users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Click the button below to contact the Vidhai AI developer.
  2. Use the subject "Vidhai AI Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include the email address associated with your account. Do not include your password or PIN.
  4. The developer will verify the request and explain the next steps.

Request Account Deletion

What data will be deleted?

After a request is verified and processed, the developer will delete the account and associated personal data covered by the deletion request, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Data retention

Information that must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons may be kept only as required. Any such retention must follow the applicable privacy policy and legal requirements.

Contact

Vidhai AI account deletion requests can be sent to the developer using the email link on this page.
